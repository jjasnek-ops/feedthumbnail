YOUTUBE CREATOR GUIDE 01

YouTube thumbnail size

For a video thumbnail, YouTube currently recommends 3840 × 2160 pixels, a 16:9 aspect ratio, and a minimum width of 640 pixels. YouTube lists JPG and PNG among the supported image formats.

VIDEO THUMBNAIL

The numbers to check before upload

01

Canvas: 3840 × 2160 pixels is YouTube's current recommendation for video thumbnails.

02

Shape: Use 16:9 for a standard video thumbnail. The official page says to try this aspect ratio for videos.

03

SURFACE CHECK

What changes on mobile and Shorts?

Video thumbnails

Keep the 16:9 canvas

YouTube's help page recommends 16:9 for videos. It does not give a fixed numeric phone-or-TV crop map for standard video thumbnails.

Vertical video rule

Home, Explore, and subscriptions can use 4:5

For vertical videos with a 16:9 custom thumbnail, YouTube says an auto-generated 4:5 thumbnail replaces it on those surfaces. The custom thumbnail still appears on the watch feed, watch history, and non-mobile platforms.

Shorts

YouTube Shorts thumbnail size

What is the YouTube Shorts thumbnail size? YouTube recommends 2160 × 3840 pixels for Shorts, with a minimum height of 640 pixels. Custom Shorts thumbnails are currently added in YouTube Studio on a computer.

SOURCE NOTES

What YouTube Help does and does not specify

FOUND Video recommendation, minimum width, ratios, listed formats, upload limits, Shorts resolution, and the 4:5 vertical-video surface rule are stated on the official thumbnail help page.

NOT FOUND A fixed pixel crop boundary for a standard thumbnail on TV or phone surfaces is not stated on that page, so this guide does not invent one.
