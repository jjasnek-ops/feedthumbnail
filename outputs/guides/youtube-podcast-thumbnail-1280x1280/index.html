YOUTUBE THUMBNAIL SIZE · PODCAST PIXEL CHECK

YouTube podcast thumbnail size: 1280 × 1280 is the current recommendation

For a YouTube podcast show's square playlist thumbnail, start with 1280 × 1280 pixels. YouTube's current podcast setup Help page recommends that square canvas. Its general thumbnail Help page supplies the matching 1:1 ratio for podcast playlists. The wording is a recommendation, not a published minimum or maximum, so keep the source scope visible when you document your export.

Official source: Create a podcast in YouTube Studio - YouTube Help, reviewed 2026-10-16.

THE NUMERIC ANSWER

What size does YouTube recommend for a podcast thumbnail?

The current YouTube podcast setup page treats a podcast show as a playlist and its episodes as videos. In the podcast details workflow, it asks for a square podcast thumbnail. Later in the same workflow, it gives the numeric recommendation: 1280 × 1280 pixels. That closes the pixel gap left by the general thumbnail page without changing the underlying shape rule.

“We recommend 1280x1280 pixels for your square podcast thumbnail.”

— YouTube Help, Create a podcast in YouTube Studio

Read the sentence precisely. It says “recommend,” not “require,” “minimum,” or “maximum.” The source also places the instruction in the podcast Details flow, where a creator uploads the show's square artwork. It does not claim that every podcast-related image, every episode thumbnail, or every account-specific upload route must use the same canvas.

WHAT THE NUMBER APPLIES TO

Is 1280 × 1280 for the playlist cover or for every episode?

The number belongs to the square podcast thumbnail in the podcast details flow. That is the show's playlist-level cover. YouTube's same page explains that episodes are videos inside the playlist, so the cover and the episode image should be treated as separate assets even when they share a visual identity.

Podcast show coverPrepare a square 1280 × 1280 canvas as the current official recommendation for the playlist-level artwork.
Episode video thumbnailUse the video-thumbnail workflow and its 16:9 guidance when the target is an individual episode video.
Podcast episode mediaYouTube represents a podcast episode with a video in the show's playlist; the square cover is not a replacement for that video asset.

This distinction prevents a common export mistake: taking a 16:9 video thumbnail preset and calling it podcast cover art. A 1280 × 720 file may be a sensible video canvas, but it is not the square podcast-playlist recommendation. Conversely, a square cover does not automatically become the correct thumbnail for every episode video.

WHY THE TWO HELP PAGES FIT TOGETHER

Does the general YouTube thumbnail page contradict 1280 × 1280?

No. The general customised thumbnails Help page answers the aspect-ratio question: use 1:1 for podcast playlists instead of 16:9. The podcast setup page answers the more specific pixel question: YouTube recommends 1280 × 1280 for the square podcast thumbnail. One page supplies the shape; the other supplies a current starting canvas.

The general page also separates video and podcast upload contexts when it lists file-size limits. It says podcast thumbnails have a 10 MB mobile limit and a 50 MB desktop limit in the checked section. Those limits describe the upload route and device context; they do not turn 1280 × 1280 into a hard dimension rule. If the Studio interface changes, re-check the current Help page and the upload form before treating an old note as universal.

A SQUARE-FIRST UPLOAD CHECKLIST

How should you prepare the file?

  1. Confirm the target. Select the podcast show's Details page or an individual episode's video settings before opening a design template.
  2. Use the current recommendation. For the square show cover, make the working canvas 1280 × 1280 pixels. Keep the title, face, and logo inside a comfortable inner margin so small previews do not cut off the important parts.
  3. Export a practical upload format. The general YouTube Help page names JPG and PNG as accepted thumbnail formats. Keep the file within the current device-specific limit shown by YouTube, and do not infer a dimension limit from a file-size limit.
  4. Keep episode art separate. If the show also publishes standard videos, prepare those episode thumbnails on the 16:9 path and check the video-specific guidance.
  5. Verify in YouTube Studio. The final upload and display check belongs in the account's current Studio interface. A local file check can confirm pixels, format, and size; it cannot confirm the account's saved cover or every YouTube surface.

This checklist combines the current first-party recommendation with a clear tool boundary. It does not estimate search volume, traffic, ranking, or a hidden acceptance rule.

QUESTIONS

YouTube thumbnail size and podcast questions

What is the recommended YouTube podcast thumbnail size?

For the square podcast show thumbnail, YouTube's current podcast setup Help page recommends 1280 × 1280 pixels. The general thumbnail page supplies the matching 1:1 ratio.

Is 1280 × 1280 a hard minimum?

Not from the wording checked here. YouTube says it recommends 1280 × 1280; the page does not label that number as a minimum or maximum.

Is a podcast thumbnail 16:9?

The podcast show's playlist-level thumbnail is square, or 1:1. An individual episode is a video and can follow the separate video-thumbnail workflow.

Can I use 1280 × 720 for the podcast cover?

That is the current Feed Thumbnail resizer's 16:9 video canvas, not the official square podcast-cover recommendation. Confirm the asset target before exporting.

Are 3000 × 3000 or 1080 × 1080 required?

No. Those numbers appear as personal outcomes in the checked Reddit discussion. They are not presented as universal YouTube requirements.

Can Feed Thumbnail confirm my podcast upload?

No. It can document the local file path, but it does not sign in to YouTube Studio or verify an account-specific cover after upload.
