YOUTUBE THUMBNAIL SIZE · PODCAST PLAYLIST CHECK

YouTube thumbnail size for podcast playlists: use 1:1

Use a square 1:1 image for a YouTube podcast playlist cover. YouTube's current Help page says podcast playlists should use a 1:1 thumbnail instead of 16:9. The page does not state a fixed pixel dimension in that section, so do not turn 1280 × 720, 3840 × 2160, 3000 × 3000, or 1080 × 1080 into an official podcast requirement without another source.

Official source: Add customised thumbnails on YouTube - YouTube Help, reviewed 2026-10-15.

THE CURRENT OFFICIAL WORDING

What does YouTube say about a podcast playlist thumbnail?

The current YouTube Help page gives different aspect-ratio guidance for different thumbnail jobs. For videos, it says to try 16:9. For Shorts, it says to try 9:16. For podcast playlists, it says to use 1:1 instead of 16:9. That is the official answer to the ratio question: a podcast playlist cover is square.

Use a 1:1 thumbnail for podcast playlists instead of 16:9.

— YouTube Help, Image size and resolution

The same section gives numeric recommendations for video and Shorts thumbnails, plus a minimum width for videos and a minimum height for Shorts. It does not pair the podcast-playlist 1:1 instruction with a numeric width-and-height recommendation. A careful answer should keep the published ratio and the missing pixel value separate.

DO NOT MIX THE TWO ASSETS

Is a podcast playlist cover the same as an episode thumbnail?

Not automatically. The official wording names a podcast playlist when it gives the 1:1 exception. It separately describes video thumbnails with a 16:9 recommendation. That means a creator should identify the upload target before choosing a canvas: the podcast playlist cover is the square asset, while an individual episode can still have a video thumbnail workflow.

A SQUARE-FIRST WORKFLOW

How should you prepare the image?

  1. Confirm the target. Decide whether the file is for the podcast playlist cover or for an individual video. Do not let a familiar 16:9 thumbnail preset decide that for you.
  2. Start with 1:1 for the playlist cover. Keep the artwork square and place the title, face, or logo away from the edge so the design remains readable when it is shown small.
  3. Do not invent the pixel requirement. Record the official ratio, then check the current upload interface for any account- or route-specific constraint. A community report that 3000 × 3000 worked is evidence of one user's outcome, not an official minimum or maximum.
  4. Keep episode art separate. If the same podcast also has standard videos, prepare their thumbnails using the video guidance and the 16:9 tool path where appropriate.
  5. Verify in Studio. The final upload and display check belongs in YouTube's own product. Feed Thumbnail can prepare or inspect a local image, but it cannot report a Studio result.

This workflow combines the official ratio with a documented first-party tool boundary. It does not estimate search volume, traffic, or a hidden pixel limit.

What is the YouTube podcast thumbnail size?

Is 1280 × 720 the podcast playlist size?

Do not treat it as the podcast answer. It is a common 16:9 video-thumbnail canvas and the current Feed Thumbnail resizer target, while the official podcast-playlist instruction is 1:1.

Is 3000 × 3000 required?
