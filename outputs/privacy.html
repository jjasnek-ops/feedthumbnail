SITE POLICY 04

Privacy, in plain English.

Thumbnail Tester is designed to keep the image you preview on your own device. This page describes the current behavior, Google Analytics measurement, affiliate links, and the advertising plan.

01

Your image stays in the browser

When you choose or paste an image, the page uses it for the local preview and export flow. The tool does not upload the image to a server or send it to an image-processing API.

02

No account is required

You can use the tool without registering or signing in. The static site does not ask for a password, payment information, or an access token.

03

Hosting and site requests

The pages are delivered as a static site through Cloudflare Pages. The hosting provider may process ordinary request data needed to deliver the site, protect the service, and keep it available under its own policies.

04

Google Analytics measurement

The site uses Google Analytics to measure page views and general website usage. Google may use cookies or similar technologies and process usage information under its own policies. Read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services for Google's explanation.

05

Affiliate links and advertising

The site may include affiliate links to third-party products and services, including Corel. If you follow an affiliate link and make a qualifying purchase, we may receive a commission at no extra cost to you. No third-party ads are displayed in the current tool. The site may add third-party advertising in the future; if that happens, this page will be updated to describe the change.

06

Changes to this page

If the tool changes its analytics, advertising, forms, or any other data practice, this page will be revised to describe the new behavior.
