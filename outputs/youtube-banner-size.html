YOUTUBE CHANNEL GUIDE 02

YouTube banner size

For a channel banner, YouTube lists a minimum upload size of 2048 × 1152 pixels and a 16:9 aspect ratio. Keep important text and logos inside the 1235 × 338 pixel safe area at that minimum size.
