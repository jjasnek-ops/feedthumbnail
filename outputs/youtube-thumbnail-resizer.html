QUESTIONS

YouTube thumbnail compressor and resizer FAQ

What does this YouTube thumbnail size converter export?

It exports one 1280 × 720 JPG from the local canvas. The selected crop, padding, or stretch treatment is included in the saved file.

What happens when the JPG is too large?

The browser tries lower JPEG quality settings locally and updates the file-size readout. It does not send the image to a server.

Which option keeps the whole image?

Choose Fit image. It adds padding where the source aspect ratio does not fill the 16:9 frame.

Can I use this thumbnail resizer without an account?

Yes. There is no login and no daily usage counter. The tool handles one local image at a time.

Is this a resize image for YouTube thumbnail service?

Yes, but the work stays in your browser: select a local file, preview the 1280 × 720 result, then save the checked JPG.
