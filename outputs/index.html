QUICK ANSWERS

What can you check before publishing?

PURPOSE

What does Thumbnail Tester check?

It shows one thumbnail and one video title in simulated desktop and phone feed layouts so you can check the composition before publishing.

LOCAL HANDLING

Does the image leave your device?

No. The preview and export run in this browser, and the selected image is not uploaded to the site.

EXPORT

Can you save the preview?

Yes. Use Save current view to export the visible desktop or phone preview as a PNG.
